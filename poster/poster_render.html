Your Heartbeat Is Your Password

Lightweight Deep ECG Biometrics for Edge Authentication

Course: EEE G513: Machine Learning for Electrical Engineers  ·  Instructor: Dr. Ritesh Vyas  ·  Team: Orion  ·  Institution: BITS Pilani
Problem & Executive Summary
  • Vulnerability of Conventional Modalities: Fingerprint and facial biometrics are vulnerable to spoofing (synthetic masks, silicone prints) and lack inherent vitality guarantees.
  • Vitality & Distinctiveness of ECG: Cardiac electrophysiology provides intrinsic liveness. Anatomical heart geometry, chest orientation, and conduction velocity confer unique P-QRS-T waveforms.
  • Edge Hardware Target: Resource-constrained microcontrollers (≤100 KB RAM, ≤1 ms latency) such as the $1 Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040) or ESP32.
  • Benchmark Integrity: Evaluated on the open PhysioNet ECG-ID Database (89 subjects, 7,875 beats) on a strictly recording-disjoint split.
Proposed Pipeline & Methodology
  1. Signal Filtering: Raw lead-I ECG zero-phase filtered with a 4th-order Butterworth bandpass (0.5–40.0 Hz) to eliminate baseline wander and high-frequency EMG noise.
  2. QRS Detection: Squared derivative energy envelope with adaptive prominence detects R-peaks. Windows of 300 samples (0.6 s: 0.2 s pre-R to 0.4 s post-R) are segmented and z-score normalized.
  3. Augmentation Engine: On-the-fly random temporal roll (±8 samples), amplitude scaling (±10%), and dynamic heart-rate resampling jitter (±5%).
  4. Architecture (ECG_ResNet_SE):
    • Depthwise-separable convolutions cut parameter count by 70%.
    • Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) channel attention adaptively reweights individual-specific wave components (QRS vs. P/T wave).
    • 48-dimensional normalized latent biometric embedding space.
  5. Multi-Task Hybrid Objective: $$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{CrossEntropy}} + 0.5 \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{ArcFace}}$$ Simultaneously optimizes 1:N closed-set identification and unit-hypersphere metric clustering for 1:1 verification.

Multi-Beat Decision Window Ablation

In operational access control, authentication decisions integrate consecutive heartbeats:

Within 5 consecutive heartbeats (~4 seconds), identification accuracy matches published academic state-of-the-art benchmarks on ECG-ID while fitting inside 68 KB.

Prototype Replay & Hardware Feasibility
  • Offline Replay Prototype (scripts/demo_replay.py): Replaying unseen test recordings against enrolled templates achieves a 97.8% Genuine Accept Rate (87/89 subjects).
  • Microcontroller Deployability (Raspberry Pi Pico / RP2040):
    • Model requires ~15 KB raw weights and ~8 KB activation RAM.
    • Consumes less than 10% of the RP2040's 264 KB SRAM.
    • Projected inference latency: ~40 ms at 133 MHz clock (< 50 mW power draw).
  • Honest Limitations: Tested on a strictly recording-disjoint holdout. Multi-month cross-day validation remains constrained by dataset demographics (20 multi-day vs. 69 same-day subjects).
