Summary & Highlights

This project addresses spoof-resistant, privacy-preserving biometric authentication on low-cost microcontrollers using single-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) waveforms. Following an empirical strategy that progressively benchmarked classical AC/DCT SVMs, recurrent LSTMs, and 1D-CNNs, our proposed ECG_ResNet_SE architecture achieves 90.87% single-beat Rank-1 accuracy and cuts verification Equal Error Rate from 13.11% (baseline) down to 4.50%, with a decidability index of $d' = 4.13$ and 93.62% TAR at 1.0% FAR. Ensembling 5 heartbeats (~4 s) delivers 95.51% identification accuracy. Furthermore, extending to a Dual-Stream architecture fusing voltage morphology with phase velocity ($dV/dt$) pushes 8-beat accuracy beyond 97% (97.19%) and reaches 97.75% across 10 beats. Deployed in pure C99 on a $5 Raspberry Pi Pico WH at 250 MHz, the system executes inference in 300.05 ms within 161.6 KB SRAM with zero runtime Flash writes.

Introduction & Progressive Strategy

Motivation: Fingerprints and facial recognition lack intrinsic liveness and remain vulnerable to silicone molds or synthetic deepfakes. Cardiac electrophysiology offers an inherent vitality guarantee rooted in thoracic anatomical geometry. However, microcontrollers lack FPUs and suffer from ambient 50 Hz power-line hum.

  • Systematic Benchmark Odyssey: Progressively benchmarked AC/DCT+SVM, recurrent LSTM, and shallow 1D-CNNs under a strictly recording-disjoint holdout split across 89 identities, diagnosing theoretical failure modes.
  • Residual Attention & Metric Learning: Engineered ECG_ResNet_SE with Squeeze-and-Excitation channel attention and ArcFace loss to enforce tight hyperspherical clusters.
  • Dual-Stream Research Frontier (>97%): Developed a 2-channel Dual-Stream model ($[x, \Delta x]$) capturing instantaneous Purkinje velocity ($dV/dt$), demonstrating that ensembling 8 beats pushes accuracy to 97.19% and 10 beats to 97.75%.
  • Bare-Metal Dual-Core Deployment: Implemented RP2040 dual-core AMP firmware, validating zero-shot enrollment and live AD8232 analog authentication.
Theoretical Foundations & Metric Learning

Lead Field Dipole Projection: Cardiac vectorcardiography projects the spatial dipole $\mathbf{J}(t)$ onto lead axes: $V_{\text{lead}}(t) = \int \mathbf{L}(\mathbf{r}) \cdot \mathbf{J}(\mathbf{r}, t) \, d^3\mathbf{r}$.

ArcFace Metric Loss: To maximize inter-class separation and minimize intra-class spread for open-set verification, we enforce an angular margin penalty $m=0.285\text{ rad}$ on a hypersphere of radius $s=16.0$:

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ArcFace}} = -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \log \frac{e^{s \cdot \cos(\theta_{y_i} + m)}}{e^{s \cdot \cos(\theta_{y_i} + m)} + \sum_{j \neq y_i} e^{s \cdot \cos \theta_j}}$

Methodology & Edge Architecture
Figure 1: End-to-end edge ECG architecture. Core 0 performs hard real-time 500 Hz ADC acquisition, causal IIR filtering, and SQI gating; Core 1 executes the ResNet-SE engine in 300.05 ms.
  1. Preprocessing: 500 Hz ADC timer ISR → causal Butterworth (0.5–40 Hz) → 50 Hz notch filter ($Q=15$) → Pan-Tompkins QRS detection → 5-point SQI validation.
  2. Model Design: 1D stem ($k=15, s=2$) → 3 residual stages ($k=7, 5, 3$) with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention ($r=4$) → 48-D hyperspherical embedding (14,867 params, ~0.45 MFLOPs).
  3. The Dual-Stream Decision: Raw voltage captures wave amplitudes; the discrete difference $\Delta x[n] = x[n] - x[n-1]$ explicitly encodes Purkinje conduction slew rate ($dV/dt$), fusing phase-space coordinates to maximize classification power.

Hardware Capabilities & Model Limitations
  • Compute Profile & Latency Bottlenecks: At 250 MHz, the Cortex-M0+ executes 0.45 MFLOPs in 300.05 ms (~75M clock cycles) due to GCC software floating-point emulation (80–120 cycles per operation without hardware FPU). However, for 1 beat/s ECG signals, 300 ms utilizes only 30% of Core 1's temporal duty cycle, allowing real-time processing of every heartbeat without queuing.
  • Memory Envelope & Flash Longevity: The single-stream model fits comfortably within the 264 KB SRAM limit: static activation buffers consume 72.0 KB in BSS, and live 48-D user templates occupy only 192 bytes each (1.5 KB for 8 users). Zero Flash writes occur at runtime, preserving NOR Flash endurance indefinitely. Dual-Stream increases activation memory to 97 KB and latency to ~420 ms, making single-stream ECG_ResNet_SE the optimal trade-off for 3-second edge unlock.
  • Heart-Rate Drift Limits: Natural variations of $\pm 5\text{ BPM}$ incur minimal loss (<2.5%), but severe tachycardia (>85 BPM) reduces single-beat accuracy to 42.31% unless dynamic tempo normalization is applied.
  • Impostor Rejection on Live Bench: Setting threshold $\tau = 0.68$ and gallery scope isolation achieved 0/5 false accepts (100% rejection) for impostor P2 while keeping 5/5 accepts (100% pass) for genuine user P1.
Conclusion & Future Work
  • Engineered an ultra-lightweight, end-to-end deep learning ECG biometric authentication pipeline running in real time on an ARM Cortex-M0+ microcontroller without an FPU.
  • Achieved 95.51% 5-beat identification accuracy, a 4.50% verification EER, and a decidability index of $d' = 4.13$ under strictly recording-disjoint evaluation, with Dual-Stream surpassing 97.19% at 8 beats.
  • Demonstrated complete physical integration with AD8232 analog front-end, Wi-Fi AP streaming, and robust zero-shot live enrollment within a 161.6 KB SRAM envelope.
  • Future Work: ARM Helium vectorization on Cortex-M33 (RP2350) for <15 ms latency, dry capacitive electrodes for consumer wearables, and longitudinal multi-month drift modeling.
